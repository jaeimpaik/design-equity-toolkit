Welcome!

This Team, Can – Designing with Equity toolkit website comes in conjunction with the physical Team, Can tin can. This website shares more details of each of the design equity tool instructions. Use this website together with the physical cards in the best way you see fit. You can add some fun by playing the cards as a game, doing team bonding activities with them, or focusing on a specific design step to do an equity exercise.

Regardless of what format you choose to use the cards and website, make sure to have fun!
